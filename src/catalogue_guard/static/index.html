AUDITABLE BY DESIGN

Sources, not assumptions.

Three public source families. Independent observations, explicit uncertainty and checks against accidental double-counting.

How matching works

GTIN lengths and check digits are validated, then codes are represented as 14-character strings. Recall identifiers are extracted conservatively from product blocks. Unparsed blocks remain in a separate table.

A code identifies a candidate relationship. Disjoint name tokens flag a possible description conflict; this simple rule is a review aid, not a validated semantic classifier.

What the figures mean

Prices are observations, not sales. Notices are historical reports, not current stock warnings. Comparisons keep currencies and price units separate. Product metadata may describe a different point in time from a price or notice.

No customer revenue, cost savings or product-safety outcome is claimed.

What is checked

One catalogue row per valid canonical code. Prices and recalls are aggregated before joining. Matched totals reconcile to their source tables. Rebuilding uses a fresh staging database rather than appending duplicate snapshots.

Licenses & attribution

Product information and price observations: Open Food Facts contributors, ODbL. Recall notices: RappelConso / French public authorities, Open Licence 2.0. Code: MIT.

Source URLs and SHA-256 checksums are recorded with each download. Receipt images and contributor identities are excluded from the app.
